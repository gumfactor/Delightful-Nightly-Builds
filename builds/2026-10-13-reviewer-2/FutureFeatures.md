# Future Features
1. Real-paper mode: pull recent PubMed abstracts and have Claude find and grade the real weaknesses, as a teaching tool for journal club.
2. Class mode: export a shareable seed and a score-sheet so students play the same set and the instructor sees per-flaw accuracy.
3. Spaced repetition: resurface specific cases missed before, not only flaw types.
4. Two-step answers: tap the sentence containing the flaw, then name it.
5. Fix-it round: after naming the flaw, choose the best design change (independent localizer, preregistration, control group).
6. Domain packs: forensic neuroscience, stress research, and a Canada List data-quality pack.
7. Have Claude grade free-text critiques rather than multiple choice.
