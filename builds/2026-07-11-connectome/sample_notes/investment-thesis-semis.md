# Semiconductor Capex Thesis — Working Notes

Thesis: AI infrastructure buildout drives a multi-year semiconductor capex
cycle, but the winners shift over time — first the compute layer, then the
memory/interconnect layer, then the power/cooling layer as data center scale
becomes the constraint.

Things to track over time (accountability, not just prediction):
- Capex guidance revisions from the largest hyperscalers, quarter over
  quarter — rising guidance is a leading indicator for the whole chain.
- Memory pricing cycles, since memory has historically been the most
  cyclical and hardest-to-time part of this thesis.
- Power/cooling as the "picks and shovels behind the picks and shovels" —
  under-covered relative to compute, worth revisiting each quarter.

Note to self: write down the thesis and the price *at the time of writing*,
not just the conclusion — the value of a thesis journal is being able to
check your reasoning against what actually happened, not just what you
believed.
