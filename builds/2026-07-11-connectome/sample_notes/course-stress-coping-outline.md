# Stress and Coping — Course Outline Draft

Rough structure for the Stress and Coping course this term. Trying to build
each unit around a mechanism, then a coping strategy that targets that
mechanism specifically, rather than a generic list of "stress management
tips."

Units:
1. The stress response as an adaptive system — allostasis vs. allostatic
   load, why chronic activation becomes the problem rather than the response
   itself.
2. Cognitive appraisal — primary and secondary appraisal, why the same
   stressor produces different physiological responses across people.
3. Social support and affective buffering — ties directly into the empathy
   and affective neuroscience research program, good opportunity to bring in
   real lab findings as case studies.
4. Coping strategy taxonomy — problem-focused vs. emotion-focused, and where
   each is actually adaptive vs. maladaptive depending on controllability of
   the stressor.

Book tie-in: this course structure is basically the outline for the public-
facing book — worth keeping the language accessible from the start rather
than writing an academic version first and simplifying later.
