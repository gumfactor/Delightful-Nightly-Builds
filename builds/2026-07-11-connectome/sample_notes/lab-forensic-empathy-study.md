# Forensic Empathy Study — Design Notes

Planning the next behavioral study on empathy and psychopathic traits in a
forensic sample. Core question: does affective empathy deficit predict
recidivism risk independent of cognitive empathy scores?

Design considerations:
- Use a validated affective empathy scale alongside a cognitive perspective-
  taking task, not a single combined empathy score — the two dissociate in
  prior forensic samples.
- Behavioral RT task as an implicit measure to triangulate against self-report,
  since forensic populations have known self-report distortion issues.
- Need a clear exclusion protocol for chance-level performance and
  anticipatory guessing on the RT task, and for straight-lining on the
  self-report battery.

Open question: whether stress reactivity (cortisol or HRV) should be added as
a covariate — ties into the broader stress-and-coping research program and
could strengthen the grant narrative around affective neuroscience mechanisms.
