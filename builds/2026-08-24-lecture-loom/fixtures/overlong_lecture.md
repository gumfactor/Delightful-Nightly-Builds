# Forensic Neuroscience Overview

By the end of this lecture, students will be able to describe three major paradigms in forensic neuroscience.

## History
- Phineas Gage and early lesion studies
- Emergence of neurolaw as a field
- Key court cases citing neuroimaging evidence

## Psychopathy and the Brain
- Reduced amygdala reactivity to distress cues
- Prefrontal-limbic connectivity differences
- The role of callous-unemotional traits
- Debates over biological determinism in sentencing
- Twin studies and heritability estimates

## Neuroimaging in Court
- Admissibility standards for brain evidence
- Group-to-individual inference problem
- Case law: how courts have used fMRI and structural MRI
