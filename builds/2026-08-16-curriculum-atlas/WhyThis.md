## The Decision

Scanning the last 10 builds for topic saturation: investing/markets appeared twice (Portfolio Lab 08-09, Quarter Call 08-11) — not over the 2-build saturation threshold, but close enough to steer away from a third. Papers/citations already account for nearly half of Category C's own history, so a fourth paper-discovery tool would be the least novel option available even though it was the first idea considered.

PROFILE.md names three specific courses the user currently teaches (Stress and Coping, Social Affective Neuroscience, AI Applications for Psychologists) plus "developing new AI-focused university courses" and "updating neuroscience curriculum" as active work, and "Course material creation" as an explicit friction point. Across all 66 prior builds, not one touches teaching materials directly — CircuitLab (07-13) is a Learning Aid *for the user's students/teaching content* but built as a quiz trainer over hand-authored neuroanatomy content, not a knowledge base over the user's own syllabi. That is a real, named, zero-coverage gap, and it fits Category C's actual shape (a personal knowledge base over the user's own documents) better than it fits any other category.

## What I Hope the User Gets From This

1. A concrete, honest answer to "am I teaching the same concept in two different courses on purpose, or by accident?" — something no single syllabus file can answer on its own.
2. A gap check against their own stated learning objectives before a term starts, using their actual materials rather than memory.
3. A standing record of how a course's concept set drifts from one term to the next, without having to diff old syllabi by hand.
