Trial Averaging & Signal-to-Noise

Every single EEG trial is mostly noise. Averaging many trials cancels the random noise while the stimulus-locked ERP component (a P300-like bump) stays constant — that's the entire logic behind ERP research.

P300 Concealed Information Test

The Concealed Information Test (Guilty Knowledge Test) presents crime-relevant probe items among plausible irrelevant items. A person who recognizes a probe as significant tends to show a larger P300. This is a teaching simulation of the statistical methodology — not a claim that any real-world lie-detection test is infallible.
