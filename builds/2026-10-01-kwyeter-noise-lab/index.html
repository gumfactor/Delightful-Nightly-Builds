Playback

Start quiet and raise the volume slowly. Sustained listening above about 70 dB(A) can damage hearing, and masking noise should sit just below the level of the sound it covers.

Tinnitus pitch match

Slide until the tone matches the pitch of your tinnitus, then centre a 1-octave notch on it. Notched-noise listening is an experimental approach studied in research, not a medical treatment. Talk to an audiologist before relying on it. The tone plays quietly, keep it that way.
